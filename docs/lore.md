# Aeternum: the story

Aeternum is a world that only lives while people move through it. Long ago the
Ever-Flame at its heart dimmed, and a grey **Stillness** began to spread over every
road no one walked. Where it settles, **rifts** tear open and spill monsters.

You are a **Wayfarer**. Every step you take in the real world carries a spark back
into Aeternum. New Wayfarers start **Unkindled**. As your distance grows you climb
from rank E to S, and the few who carry the flame farthest become **Grandmasters**.
Your titles come from how you run and the element you choose, for example
Emberheart, Void Pilgrim or Harvest Keeper.

Naming rule: new items, titles and places should come from this story (embers,
roads, stillness, rifts, the elements). Don't borrow names or terms from existing
series, such as "Gates", "Hunters", "Awakened" or "Monarchs".

Code note: the top rank is still stored as `Sovereign` so saved data keeps working.
`rankLabel()` in `src/types/index.ts` shows it as Grandmaster.
