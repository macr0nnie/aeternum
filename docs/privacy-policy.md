# Aeternum Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Aeternum ("the app", "we") is a fitness role-playing game. Your real-world workouts power your in-game hero. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and how you can delete it.

## Data we collect

| Data | Where it comes from | Why we use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Workouts, steps, distance and heart rate** | Health Connect (Android), only after you grant permission | To validate your runs and turn them into in-game stats, loot and progress |
| **Location** | Your device's GPS, only while you use the world map | To show the map around you and let you claim and visit territories |
| **Username and hero choices** | What you enter in the app | To show your hero to you and other players |
| **Account ID** | Created automatically when you start playing (guest account) | To save your progress |
| **Crash reports** | The app, if it crashes | To find and fix bugs |

We only read health data that you allow in Health Connect. We read the most recent workout from the last 48 hours when you sync. We don't write any data to Health Connect.

## What other players can see

Your username, rank and hero are visible to other players, for example on the leaderboard and in parties. **Territories you claim appear on the shared world map at the place where you claimed them.**

## How we use and share data

- Health data is used **only** to provide the game's fitness features. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with third parties, except the service providers below that run the game for us.
- Aeternum's use of information received from Health Connect follows the [Health Connect Permissions policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12991134), including its Limited Use requirements.
- **Service providers:** game data is stored with Supabase (database and hosting). Crash reports are sent to Sentry. They process data only on our behalf.
- We don't show ads and don't sell any personal data.

## Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. To delete everything, open **Settings → Delete Account** in the app. That permanently removes your account, hero, runs, territories and all other game data from our servers. You can also revoke Aeternum's access to your health data at any time in Health Connect, and its location access in Android settings.

## Children

Aeternum is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Significant changes will be shown in the app.
